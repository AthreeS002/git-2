Mini Hedgehog

Mini hedgehogs, commonly known as African pygmy hedgehogs, are small, spiny, nocturnal exotic pets that typically measure 7 to 9 inches long and weigh between 250 and 600 grams.

Physical Traits and Behaviour

  • Spines: Their backs are covered in permanent, sharp quills, while their undersides have soft white fur.
  • Defense: When threatened, they roll into a tight, prickly ball using strong core muscles.
  • Habits: They are strictly nocturnal, meaning they sleep during the day and become active at night.
  • Anointing: They exhibit a strange behavior called "anointing," where they chew on new items and spread fothy saliva across their quills.
