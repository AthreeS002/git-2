Funfact About Mini Hedgehog

Mini hedgehog, or African pygmy hedgehogscan carry up to 7,000 sharp quills and roll into a tight protective ball when scared. perform a bizarre behavior called "self-anointing," where they lick a new scent, foam up the saliva in their mouths, and paste it all over their own spines. African pygmy hedgehogs grow only 7 to 9 inches long and fit neatly into the palm of your hand, about the size of a grapefruit when rolled into a ball.

Fun Facts About Mini Hedgehog

About Description
Baby names: Baby hedgehogs are called hoglets.
Sleep schedule: They are nocturnal, meaning they sleep during the day and wake up at night.
Diet: In the wild, they eat bugs and worms, but pet mini hedgehogs often eat high-protein cat food.
